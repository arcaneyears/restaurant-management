About Saffron Table

Ten years of cooking on Dostyk Avenue and a management system we are happy to show to anyone who asks.

Our story

From a ten-seat bistro to a full service kitchen

In 2016 two cooks rented a narrow room with ten seats, one oven and a handwritten menu that changed with whatever the market had that morning. The food was good, but the second week showed us the real problem: nobody knew who ordered the vegetables.

So we wrote it down. Then we wrote down the prep lists, the portion costs, the cleaning rotation and the shift plan. Today Saffron Table seats seventy guests and employs twenty-four people, and that same habit of writing things down is the reason the kitchen still feels calm at eight in the evening.

  1. 2016 The first room.

    Ten seats, two cooks and a market menu written every morning.

  2. 2019 The move to Dostyk Avenue.

    Seventy seats, an open kitchen counter and our first sous chef.

  3. 2022 The events department.

    Catering for up to 180 guests with a dedicated event manager.

  4. 2025 The operations desk.

    Shifts, suppliers and stock published weekly for the whole team to read.

Our dining hall
What we stand for

Four rules we do not bend

★

Seasonal first

If a vegetable is not in season nearby, it does not reach the card that week.

▢

Written standards

Every recipe, portion and cleaning task exists on paper before it exists on a plate.

♡

Fair shifts

Schedules are published seven days ahead and swaps are approved, never assumed.

♺

Low waste

Trim and offcuts go into stocks and staff meals. We weigh what we throw away.
