Our approach

A kitchen that is also a well-run workplace

Saffron Table started as a ten-seat bistro on Dostyk Avenue. What never changed is the habit of writing everything down: recipes, portion costs, delivery windows and the people responsible for each of them.

Guests taste the result as consistency. The crew feels it as fair schedules and clear expectations. We think both halves of that sentence matter equally.

  1. Produce arrives before 07:00 and is graded on arrival.
  2. Prep lists are signed off by the sous chef on shift.
  3. Every service closes with a short written debrief.
